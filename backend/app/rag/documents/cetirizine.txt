Medicine Name: Cetirizine

Generic Name:
Cetirizine Hydrochloride

Category:
Antihistamine

Clinical Uses:
Used to manage allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, allergic rhinitis, and skin allergies.

How to Take:
- Usually taken once daily.
- Can be taken with or without food.

Common Side Effects:
- Drowsiness
- Dry mouth
- Fatigue
- Dizziness

Important Precautions:
- Avoid alcohol consumption.
- Use caution while driving or operating machinery.
- Consult a doctor before use during pregnancy.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered. Skip if the next dose is near.

Drug Interaction Warnings:
- Alcohol
- Sedatives
- Sleep medications

Patient Counseling:
Take the medication at night if drowsiness occurs.
