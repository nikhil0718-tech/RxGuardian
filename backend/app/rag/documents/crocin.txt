Medicine Name: Crocin

Generic Name:
Paracetamol

Category:
Analgesic and Antipyretic

Clinical Uses:
Used for fever reduction and relief of mild to moderate pain.

How To Take:
- Take as directed.
- Can be taken with or without food.

Common Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Mild stomach discomfort
- Rare allergic reactions

Important Precautions:
- Avoid overdose.
- Do not combine with other paracetamol products.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered unless next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Alcohol
- Other paracetamol-containing medicines

Patient Counseling:
Seek medical attention if fever persists.
