DOCUMENT: Medication Adherence

Purpose:
This document explains the importance of taking medications consistently as prescribed.

Benefits of Good Adherence:

- Better treatment outcomes
- Faster recovery
- Reduced complications
- Improved disease management

Recommendations:

1. Take medicines at the prescribed times.
2. Use reminders or alarms.
3. Follow dosing instructions carefully.
4. Refill medications before they run out.
5. Consult healthcare providers before stopping treatment.

Risks of Poor Adherence:

- Treatment failure
- Disease progression
- Increased hospital visits
- Antibiotic resistance

Patient Counseling:
Consistency is one of the most important factors for successful treatment.
