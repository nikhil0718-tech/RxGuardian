DOCUMENT: Drug Interaction Awareness

Drug interactions may alter the effectiveness of medicines or increase the risk of side effects.

Recommendations:

1. Inform healthcare providers about all medications being taken.
2. Include prescription medicines, supplements, and herbal products.
3. Avoid combining medicines without medical advice.
4. Monitor for unusual symptoms after starting a new medicine.

Common Examples:
- Cetirizine + Alcohol → Increased drowsiness
- Multiple Paracetamol Products → Risk of overdose
- Certain Antibiotics + Antacids → Reduced effectiveness
