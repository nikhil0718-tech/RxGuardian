DOCUMENT: Allergy Precautions

Purpose:
This document provides guidance regarding medication allergies and allergic reactions.

Common Allergy Symptoms:

- Skin rash
- Itching
- Hives
- Swelling
- Difficulty breathing
- Wheezing

Precautions:

1. Inform healthcare providers about known allergies.
2. Read medicine labels carefully.
3. Monitor for new symptoms after starting medication.
4. Seek immediate medical care for severe reactions.

Emergency Symptoms:

- Swelling of the face or throat
- Severe breathing difficulty
- Sudden dizziness
- Loss of consciousness

Patient Counseling:
Maintain an updated list of medication allergies and share it with healthcare providers.
