Medicine Name: Combiflam

Generic Name:
Ibuprofen + Paracetamol

Category:
Pain Reliever and Anti-inflammatory

Clinical Uses:
Used for fever, headache, muscle pain, toothache, joint pain, and inflammatory conditions.

How To Take:
- Preferably take after food.
- Use as directed by your doctor.

Common Side Effects:
- Stomach irritation
- Heartburn
- Nausea
- Abdominal discomfort

Important Precautions:
- Avoid excessive use.
- Use cautiously in kidney disease.
- Avoid use with other NSAIDs.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered unless next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Blood thinners
- Aspirin
- Other NSAIDs

Patient Counseling:
Take after meals to reduce stomach irritation.
