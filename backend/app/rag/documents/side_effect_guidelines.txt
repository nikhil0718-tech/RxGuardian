DOCUMENT: Side Effect Management

Purpose:
This document provides guidance for identifying and responding to medication side effects.

Common Side Effects:

- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Stomach discomfort
- Fatigue
- Drowsiness

Mild Side Effects:
Many mild side effects improve as the body adjusts to treatment.

Seek Immediate Medical Attention If You Experience:

- Difficulty breathing
- Severe rash
- Swelling of the face, lips, or throat
- Severe chest pain
- Loss of consciousness

Patient Counseling:
Do not discontinue prescribed medications without consulting a healthcare professional unless a severe reaction occurs.
