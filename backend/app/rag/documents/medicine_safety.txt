DOCUMENT: Medicine Safety Guidelines

Purpose:
This document provides general medication safety recommendations applicable to most prescription and over-the-counter medicines.

Safety Recommendations:

1. Always take medicines exactly as prescribed.
2. Never alter dosage without consulting a healthcare professional.
3. Check expiry dates before use.
4. Store medicines according to manufacturer instructions.
5. Inform healthcare providers about allergies and ongoing treatments.
6. Do not share prescription medicines with others.
7. Read warning labels carefully.
8. Seek medical advice if unexpected side effects occur.
9. Keep medicines out of reach of children.
10. Attend follow-up appointments when required.
