Medicine Name: Pantoprazole

Generic Name:
Pantoprazole

Category:
Proton Pump Inhibitor

Clinical Uses:
Used to treat acidity, GERD, acid reflux, and stomach ulcers.

How To Take:
- Usually taken before meals.
- Swallow whole with water.

Common Side Effects:
- Headache
- Nausea
- Diarrhea
- Abdominal discomfort

Important Precautions:
- Follow prescribed duration.
- Do not self-medicate for prolonged periods.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered unless next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Certain antifungal medicines
- Iron supplements

Patient Counseling:
Lifestyle modifications can improve treatment effectiveness.

Keywords:
pantoprazole, acidity, GERD, acid reflux, heartburn, gastric ulcer
