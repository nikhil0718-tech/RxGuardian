Medicine Name: Dolo 650

Generic Name: Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)

Category:
Analgesic, Antipyretic

Clinical Uses:
Dolo 650 is commonly used to reduce fever and relieve mild to moderate pain, including headaches, body aches, muscle pain, toothache, and pain associated with viral infections.

How to Take:
- Take as prescribed by your doctor.
- Can be taken with or without food.
- Maintain adequate hydration during fever episodes.

Common Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Mild stomach discomfort
- Skin rash (rare)

Important Precautions:
- Do not exceed the recommended daily dosage.
- Excessive use may cause serious liver damage.
- Use cautiously in patients with liver disease.
- Avoid taking multiple paracetamol-containing products simultaneously.

Missed Dose:
Take the missed dose as soon as you remember. If it is almost time for the next scheduled dose, skip the missed dose. Do not take two doses together.

Drug Interaction Warnings:
- Other paracetamol-containing medicines
- Certain liver medications
- Alcohol consumption

Patient Counseling:
Seek medical attention if fever persists for more than three days or if severe symptoms develop.
