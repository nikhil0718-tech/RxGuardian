Medicine Name: Sinarest

Generic Name:
Paracetamol + Chlorpheniramine + Phenylephrine

Category:
Cold and Flu Medication

Clinical Uses:
Used for common cold symptoms including fever, nasal congestion, sneezing, and runny nose.

How To Take:
- Follow prescribed dosage.
- Can be taken after food.

Common Side Effects:
- Drowsiness
- Dry mouth
- Dizziness

Important Precautions:
- May impair alertness.
- Avoid alcohol consumption.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered unless next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Alcohol
- Sedatives
- Sleep medications

Patient Counseling:
Avoid driving if drowsiness occurs.

Keywords:
sinarest, cold medicine, runny nose, sneezing, congestion, fever, flu
