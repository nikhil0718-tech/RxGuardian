Medicine Name: Augmentin

Generic Name:
Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid

Category:
Antibiotic

Clinical Uses:
Augmentin is used to treat bacterial infections affecting the respiratory tract, skin, urinary tract, ears, and sinuses.

How to Take:
- Take exactly as prescribed.
- Preferably take with food to reduce stomach upset.
- Complete the full course even if symptoms improve.

Common Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Diarrhea
- Vomiting
- Stomach discomfort

Important Precautions:
- Inform your doctor if you have a history of penicillin allergy.
- Do not stop treatment prematurely.
- Avoid self-medication.

Missed Dose:
Take the missed dose as soon as possible. If the next dose is near, skip the missed dose. Do not double the dose.

Drug Interaction Warnings:
- Warfarin
- Methotrexate
- Certain oral contraceptives

Patient Counseling:
Seek medical advice if severe diarrhea, rash, or allergic reactions occur.
