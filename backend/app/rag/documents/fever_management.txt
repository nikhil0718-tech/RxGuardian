DOCUMENT: Fever Management Guidelines

Purpose:
This document provides general guidance for managing fever safely and effectively.

What is Fever?
Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually caused by an infection, inflammation, or other medical condition.

General Recommendations:

1. Stay well hydrated by drinking water and oral fluids regularly.
2. Get adequate rest to support recovery.
3. Monitor body temperature periodically.
4. Wear lightweight clothing and avoid overheating.
5. Use fever-reducing medicines only as directed by a healthcare professional.
6. Continue prescribed medications unless advised otherwise.

When to Seek Medical Attention:

- Fever above 39°C (102.2°F)
- Fever lasting more than three days
- Difficulty breathing
- Severe dehydration
- Persistent vomiting
- Confusion or altered mental status

Patient Counseling:
Most mild fevers resolve with proper rest, hydration, and treatment of the underlying cause.
