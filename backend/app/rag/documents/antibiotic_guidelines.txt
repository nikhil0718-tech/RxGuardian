DOCUMENT: Antibiotic Usage Guidelines

Purpose:
This document explains the safe and effective use of antibiotics.

Important Guidelines:

1. Take antibiotics exactly as prescribed.
2. Complete the full treatment course.
3. Do not skip doses.
4. Do not stop treatment early even if symptoms improve.
5. Never share antibiotics with others.
6. Do not use leftover antibiotics from previous illnesses.

Why Completing the Course Matters:
Incomplete treatment may allow bacteria to survive and contribute to antibiotic resistance.

Common Precautions:

- Inform your doctor about drug allergies.
- Inform healthcare providers about kidney or liver disease.
- Report severe diarrhea or allergic reactions.

Patient Counseling:
Antibiotics treat bacterial infections and are generally ineffective against viral illnesses such as the common cold or flu.
