Medicine Name: Azithromycin

Generic Name:
Azithromycin

Category:
Macrolide Antibiotic

Clinical Uses:
Used to treat respiratory infections, throat infections, skin infections, and certain bacterial illnesses.

How To Take:
- Take exactly as prescribed.
- Can be taken with or without food.
- Complete the entire course.

Common Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Diarrhea
- Abdominal pain
- Vomiting

Important Precautions:
- Use cautiously in patients with heart rhythm disorders.
- Inform your doctor about liver problems.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered unless next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Antacids
- Warfarin
- Heart rhythm medications

Patient Counseling:
Complete the course even if symptoms improve.

Keywords:
azithromycin, antibiotic, bacterial infection, respiratory infection, throat infection, chest infection
