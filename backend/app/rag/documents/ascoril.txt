Medicine Name: Ascoril

Generic Name:
Ambroxol + Guaifenesin + Terbutaline

Category:
Expectorant and Bronchodilator

Clinical Uses:
Used for productive cough associated with mucus production, chest congestion, bronchitis, and respiratory tract conditions.

How To Take:
- Take as prescribed by your doctor.
- Shake well before use if syrup formulation.
- Drink plenty of fluids to help loosen mucus.

Common Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Headache
- Tremors
- Increased heart rate
- Dizziness

Important Precautions:
- Use cautiously in patients with heart disease.
- Inform your doctor about asthma or hypertension.
- Do not exceed recommended dosage.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered. Skip if next dose is near.

Drug Interactions:
- Beta blockers
- Certain heart medications
- Stimulants

Patient Counseling:
Seek medical attention if breathing difficulties worsen or symptoms persist.
