DOCUMENT: Emergency Medication Guidelines

Purpose:
This document outlines situations requiring urgent medical attention.

Seek Emergency Care Immediately If:

- Medication overdose is suspected.
- Severe allergic reactions occur.
- Difficulty breathing develops.
- Severe chest pain occurs.
- Loss of consciousness occurs.
- Seizures develop.
- Uncontrolled bleeding occurs.

Possible Overdose Symptoms:

- Extreme drowsiness
- Confusion
- Vomiting
- Breathing difficulty
- Loss of consciousness

Emergency Actions:

1. Contact emergency medical services.
2. Inform healthcare providers about the medicine taken.
3. Carry medication packaging if possible.
4. Do not attempt self-treatment for serious symptoms.

Patient Counseling:
Prompt medical intervention can significantly improve outcomes during emergencies.
