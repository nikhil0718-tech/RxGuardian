Medicine Name: Zincovit

Generic Name:
Multivitamin and Multimineral Supplement

Category:
Nutritional Supplement

Clinical Uses:
Used to support immunity, nutritional deficiencies, and overall health.

How To Take:
- Take after meals.
- Follow recommended dosage.

Common Side Effects:
- Mild stomach discomfort
- Nausea

Important Precautions:
- Do not exceed recommended dosage.
- Supplements do not replace a balanced diet.

Missed Dose:
Take when remembered.

Drug Interactions:
- Certain mineral supplements
- Iron supplements

Patient Counseling:
Maintain a healthy diet alongside supplementation.
