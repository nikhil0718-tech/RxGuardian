DOCUMENT: Medicine Storage Guidelines

Purpose:
This document provides recommendations for proper medication storage.

Storage Recommendations:

1. Store medicines in a cool and dry place.
2. Keep medicines away from direct sunlight.
3. Protect medicines from moisture and humidity.
4. Keep medicines in their original packaging.
5. Ensure containers remain tightly closed.
6. Keep medicines out of reach of children and pets.

Avoid:

- Storing medicines in bathrooms.
- Exposing medicines to excessive heat.
- Using medicines after their expiry date.

Patient Counseling:
Improper storage may reduce medicine effectiveness and safety.
